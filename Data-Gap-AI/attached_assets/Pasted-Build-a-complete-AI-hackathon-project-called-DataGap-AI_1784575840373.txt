Build a complete AI hackathon project called "DataGap AI".

Project idea:
DataGap AI is an AI-powered Data Readiness Assistant that helps organizations and analysts understand whether they have enough data to answer an important business question.

The problem:
Many decisions fail because organizations analyze incomplete data. Before building reports or AI models, they need to know what information is missing.

The solution:
The user enters a business question and uploads a sample dataset (CSV/Excel). The AI analyzes the goal and available columns, then identifies missing data requirements, risks, and recommendations.
